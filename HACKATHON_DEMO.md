# CollabMind judge demo

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds. Start from a clean reset and use the pre-filled Maya profile.

## Before presenting

1. Run `npm install`, `npm run check` and `npm run build`.
2. Run `npm run dev` and open `http://localhost:3000`.
3. Open the top-right profile menu, choose **Reset demo**, and confirm.
4. Keep the browser at 100% zoom and use a width of at least 1280 px.
5. Do not open developer tools or mention API configuration. The judging build is intentionally local and deterministic.

## Exact judge flow

### 0:00–0:20 — Set up Maya

Use the pre-filled profile: Maya, Cybersecurity, YouTube, 32,000 followers, target audience **Web developers**, and the boundary **Crypto, gambling and unrelated sponsorships**. Keep the selected formats.

Click through all three onboarding steps, then **Enter workspace**.

Say:

> “CollabMind starts with five durable signals: who Maya is, who she wants to reach, how she creates, which formats she prefers and what she will not promote.”

### 0:20–0:40 — Show explainable matching

On the dashboard, open **Alex Chen** from Top opportunities. Point out the fit score, direct access to web developers, challenge-video format and concrete concept: “I hired a hacker to break my React app.”

Say:

> “This is not a creator directory sorted by followers. The concept exists because Alex owns the build audience and Maya adds a security angle he cannot offer alone.”

Close the match without starting it.

### 0:40–1:00 — Prove rejection learning

Open **Discover**, search for **Noah Williams**, then open the match. Click **Not a fit** and keep the pre-filled reason:

> “I only want deeply technical collaborations right now; avoid broad creator-business content.”

Click **Reject & teach**. Open **Memory** and show that the signal count moved from 5 to 6 and the reason appears under Boundaries.

Say:

> “A rejection is not discarded. It becomes a durable boundary and applies an explicit penalty to similar business and marketing candidates.”

### 1:00–1:35 — Move a collaboration through the pipeline

Return to **Discover**, open **Alex Chen**, then click **Start collaboration**.

In Pipeline:

1. Show the subject, outreach message and four-part brief.
2. Click **Approve & mark sent**.
3. Click **Simulate positive reply**.
4. Click **Schedule collaboration**.
5. Click **Record campaign results**.
6. Keep the deterministic result: 128,000 views, +3,400 followers, 47% non-follower reach and 8.7% engagement.
7. Click **Save results & update memory**.

Say:

> “The prototype simulates external messaging and campaign events so this judge flow is reliable. The product state and learning behavior are real and local.”

### 1:35–2:05 — Prove outcome learning

Open **Memory**. Show that the count moved from 6 to 7 and the campaign appears under Learned outcomes. Click **Ask CollabMind**.

Point out the primary audience goal, Alex as the next best collaborator, the protected boundary, campaign result, next action and exact memory evidence chips.

Say:

> “CollabMind does not only remember what Maya says. It remembers what actually worked. That outcome now boosts challenge-video formats and developer-adjacent creators in every future ranking.”

### 2:05–2:30 — Close

Close the insight, return to Discover and show the updated ranking reason **Boosted by your challenge-video result**.

Finish with:

> “Normal creator tools reset after every search. CollabMind compounds context across preferences, decisions and outcomes, turning creator collaboration into a system that improves over time.”

## Recovery shortcuts

- Press Escape to close any modal.
- Clear the search field or select **All** if a candidate is hidden.
- If the pipeline is empty, open Alex from Discover and click **Start collaboration**.
- Use the top-right profile menu → **Reset demo** to restart.
- Refreshing the page preserves the current step in LocalStorage.
